# OficiosPro Product Roadmap

## Vision US$1B

OficiosPro should become the trusted operating layer for home, business, and community services in Latin America: verified specialists, protected payments, reputation, credits, and operational workflows in one marketplace.

The long-term company goal is to build a US$1B platform by owning the trust layer for recurring service demand:

- Homes need reliable help before emergencies become expensive.
- Companies need fewer disconnected providers and more operational visibility.
- Specialists need verified demand, reputation, and better commercial tools.
- Communities need transparent maintenance and accountable execution.

## Growth Engines

1. **Organic acquisition by service and commune**
   - SEO pages for high-intent searches such as gasfíter en Las Condes, electricista SEC Santiago, mantención HVAC Providencia.
   - Specialist acquisition pages by trade.

2. **Verified specialist supply**
   - Profile verification, portfolios, certifications, ratings, and service coverage.
   - Better specialist pages increase trust and conversion.

3. **Club Hogar retention**
   - Credits, recurring maintenance, reminders, and history.
   - Makes OficiosPro useful before urgent problems happen.

4. **B2B and communities**
   - Multi-site maintenance, billing, dashboards, service history, and SLA workflows.
   - Higher contract value and recurring demand.

5. **Reputation and evidence loop**
   - Every finished job should improve search quality, specialist ranking, and conversion.
   - Photos, ratings, response time, and completion history are marketplace assets.

## 12-Week Roadmap

### Weeks 1-2: Guardrails and Measurement

- Add project validation for deployment safety.
- Protect the premium home from accidental simplification.
- Add event attributes to key CTAs.
- Define baseline metrics for specialist leads, search starts, and CTA clicks.

### Weeks 3-4: Specialist Acquisition

- Launch organic pages for specialist categories.
- Create lead capture flows for gasfíters, electricistas, HVAC, jardineros, mantención, and empresas técnicas.
- Add structured content for verification requirements and profile examples.

### Weeks 5-6: Search and Service Intent

- Improve service and commune search UX.
- Add category landing sections for high-intent services.
- Make the result cards clearer on availability, credits, verification, and evidence.

### Weeks 7-8: Trust and Conversion

- Improve Garantía OficiosPro, protected payment messaging, and completed-work proof.
- Add stronger profile pages for specialists.
- Add post-submit states and lead routing documentation.

### Weeks 9-10: Club Hogar

- Clarify credit economics and maintenance examples.
- Add plan comparison improvements.
- Add retention-oriented copy for preventive maintenance and service history.

### Weeks 11-12: Empresas and Communities

- Improve B2B lead capture.
- Add pages or sections for offices, restaurants, communities, and multi-site operations.
- Define dashboard requirements for service history, billing, and response tracking.

## Main Metrics

- Search starts from the home.
- Specialist CTA clicks.
- Specialist lead form starts and completions.
- Home-to-specialist-page click-through rate.
- Club Hogar CTA clicks.
- Empresa CTA clicks.
- Verified specialist supply by trade and commune.
- Conversion from profile view to contact/reservation.
- Completed jobs with rating and evidence.
- Repeat usage by home, company, and community accounts.

## Feature Prioritization Rules

1. Protect the premium home and deployment safety first.
2. Prefer additive improvements over rewrites.
3. Prioritize high-intent organic acquisition before broad awareness content.
4. Prioritize trust signals that reduce buyer hesitation.
5. Prioritize supply quality before demand scaling in a new commune.
6. Prefer features that create reusable marketplace data: reputation, evidence, coverage, and service taxonomy.
7. Avoid heavy frameworks unless the current static architecture clearly blocks a validated business goal.
8. Every feature should connect to at least one core metric.
